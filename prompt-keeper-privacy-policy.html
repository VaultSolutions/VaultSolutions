Privacy Policy

Prompt Keeper

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Prompt Keeper ("the extension," "we," "us") automatically saves the text prompts you copy while working with AI image or video generators, so you don't have to retype them. This policy explains what data the extension handles and, just as importantly, what it does not.

What Prompt Keeper does with your data

Your copied prompts are stored only on your own device. Prompt Keeper does not operate a server, does not collect analytics, and does not transmit anything you copy to us or to any third party.

What is stored, and where:

  • When you copy text on a page, the extension checks the text involved in that specific copy action to see if it looks like a prompt worth saving, and if so, stores that text locally in your browser's storage (chrome.storage.local / unlimitedStorage), along with the site it came from and when it was saved.
  • The extension reads this from the copy action itself (the standard browser "copy" event), not by reading your operating system's clipboard directly or at other times — it only ever sees text at the moment you actively copy it on a supported page.
  • A dedicated "saved prompts" page lets you search, sort, and export everything you've saved — this is a fuller view of the same locally-stored data described above, not a separate or additional category of data.
  • Your Founder/paid status (a simple yes/no flag).

What is never stored or transmitted:

  • Prompt Keeper does not read your clipboard's contents outside of an active copy action you perform, and never reads clipboard contents from other applications.
  • Saved prompts are not sent to any server operated by us.

Third-party services

Prompt Keeper uses ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) to process one-time payments for the paid ("Founder") tier, which in turn uses Stripe to handle the actual transaction. If you choose to purchase, ExtensionPay and Stripe receive the payment information necessary to process that transaction (such as your email address and payment details) under their own privacy policies: - ExtensionPay: https://extensionpay.com/privacy.txt - Stripe: https://stripe.com/privacy

Prompt Keeper itself only receives back a simple paid/unpaid status from ExtensionPay — not your payment details.

Permissions this extension uses, and why

  • storage / unlimitedStorage — to save your copied prompts locally, as described above.
  • downloads — to let you export your saved prompts to a file if you choose to.
  • alarms — to periodically refresh your license status.
  • activeTab — used in support of the save/export flow for the page you're currently on.
  • Content script access on all websites — Prompt Keeper is designed to work with any AI image or video generator you use, not a fixed list of named sites, so it needs to be able to detect a copy action wherever you're working. It only reacts to an actual copy event you perform; it does not read, scan, or store page content otherwise.
  • Host access to extensionpay.com — solely to check your paid status and process payments.

Data retention and deletion

Saved prompts remain in your browser's local storage until you delete them yourself or remove the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored prompts and settings.

Children's privacy

Prompt Keeper is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. If we ever change what data this extension collects or how it's used in a way that goes beyond what's described here, we will proactively disclose that change to you — not just quietly update this page — before or at the time that change takes effect. Continued use of the extension after a disclosed change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
